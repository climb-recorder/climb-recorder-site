How Climb Recorder handles projects, media, nearby connections, and support email.

Projects, records, and local files

Climb Recorder stores projects, climbs, dates, grades, attempts, sends, notes, Board likes, media links, and settings on your device. Records previously created by older versions may also remain on the device. The app and its extensions can use a shared local container for media, Board data, and related settings. The app also keeps local recovery and temporary export files, media metadata, and file fingerprints used for duplicate checks.

Camera, microphone, and Photos

  • The camera takes project photos and climbing videos. The microphone records sound with video. Photos access lets the app save recordings and use photos or videos you select.
  • Media added through the in-app picker can reference your Photos original. Media explicitly shared into the app through the iOS share sheet can be copied into its local storage, with metadata supplied by the source.
  • In Media & Storage, you can confirm a Sync to copy available originals between Photos and the app. App-to-Photos comparison can read other locally available originals to check for duplicates and requires full Photos access. The app’s PhotoKit reads are configured to use available on-device files.

Sharing, Bluetooth, and Nearby Rooms

When you export or share, you select the content and destination through iOS. Original media may carry its original metadata. A recipient or service can keep a copy under its own practices.

Bluetooth is used to find compatible Kilter controllers and send lighting commands. Nearby Rooms use local networking to discover and connect participating devices and exchange the current Board climb ID, angle, and data to verify the room session. A room invite includes the climb ID, angle, and a temporary room token. Share it with intended participants. You can leave or end the room or turn off Nearby Rooms in app settings.

Board Live Activities may show the current climb or room status on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. Your iOS settings control where these are shown.

Apple services and diagnostics

On supported versions of iOS, checking for Board data updates or requesting a download uses Apple-hosted Background Assets. The app’s download extension uses Apple’s StoreKit download interface to authorize the selected catalog pack and version. Apple processes the network requests needed to provide those services. Included and installed catalogs can be viewed offline.

The app writes local diagnostic messages for some sharing operations, such as item counts and types, the share activity and its result, and errors. This draft describes the flows identified in the candidate source. Any additional data sent by the release build, its libraries, or platform diagnostics requires release verification.

Permissions, deletion, and backups

You can change Camera, Microphone, Photos, Bluetooth, and Local Network access in iOS Settings. Turning off access stops future use of that permission and does not erase copies already saved. You can remove records and app media in Climb Recorder. Deleting a record removes its media links. When deleting a project, review the option affecting its app media. Removing media from the app leaves the Photos original in Photos.

Temporary sharing and export files can be removed during app cleanup. Unfinished recordings can be kept for recovery until you save or discard them. Removing the app, offloading it, and restoring backups are handled by iOS. Photos originals, exported copies, and existing backups may remain separately, depending on your settings and destinations. iCloud Photos and device backups are managed by your system settings.

Support and privacy requests

If you send an email, the support mailbox receives your address and the message and attachments you include. The correspondence is used to answer your request. For privacy questions or to request deletion of support correspondence, email help.konor@outlook.com.

When served online, the hosting provider can process requests to deliver them.
