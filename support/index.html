Keep your climbs and videos together

Climb Recorder was built to connect your climbing projects and attempts with the videos you recorded. You don’t have to log an attempt and film it at the same time. In a project, open Media and tap Capture or Add from Photos. To attach one or more clips to an earlier attempt, open Log, select the attempt, then tap Link, select your clips, and tap Save.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use Board?

Open Board to browse the built-in Kilter catalog. Search by climb name or filter by wall angle. Open a climb and use Wall Settings to match your physical wall. If the wall has a compatible Kilter controller, tap Light Up. If prompted, tap Find Nearby Boards and select your wall, then tap Light Up once connected.

How do I update the Board catalog?

The included catalog works offline. On iOS 26 or later, go to Settings → Board Data → Check Updates, then tap Update Full if an update is available. Downloads require internet access and are delivered by Apple. On older iOS versions, keep using the included catalog and wait for an app update that includes newer data. There is no separate server or manual download for older devices.

Do I need internet access or a server?

Projects, logs, and video links are stored on your device. Climb Recorder does not use a cloud server to store or sync your projects. Nearby Rooms connect nearby devices over the local network rather than through an internet server.

How do I keep copies in Climb Recorder and Photos?

Add from Photos links to the original in Photos; it does not make a separate app copy. To copy originals already linked to your projects, go to Settings → Media & Storage → Sync. Choose Photos → App to keep a copy in Climb Recorder, or App → Photos to save a copy to your photo library. Tap Review Missing Files, then confirm. App → Photos requires Full Access to Photos. If an original is only in iCloud, download it to your device first.

Sync copies files without deleting the originals and keeps existing attempt links inside the app. A copy in Photos is a backup of the media, not of the project. Importing that copy later won’t restore the project, log, or attempt links.
